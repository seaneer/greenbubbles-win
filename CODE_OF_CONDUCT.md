# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists to give people access to **their own** chat history. We keep
that boundary sharp: no assistance with reading other people's accounts, no
features that write to WeChat, no distribution of chat content or keys.

## Expected behaviour

- Be precise and kind in reviews; critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith, but insist on evidence for claims about WeChat internals.
- Respect privacy: never paste real conversations, contact lists, phone numbers,
  QR codes or keys into issues, PRs, or test fixtures.
- Keep discussion in the issue tracker; do not DM maintainers for support.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's chat data, credentials, or memory dumps.
- Requests or contributions that enable account takeover, message forgery,
  bulk scraping of third-party accounts, or evading WeChat's protections.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or close issues and PRs that violate this document,
and may block repeat offenders. Report concerns privately via the security
contact in `SECURITY.md`.
